# Ramen stall in the rain in Blender across three models

Gemini 4 Carbon case 67 of 223 · Head-to-Head Tests · [简体中文](#中文)

By [@colerepo](https://x.com/colerepo) · [original post](https://x.com/colerepo/status/2120698516305572227) · 2026-11-12 · [see it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/head-to-head)

- **What it is:** Same prompt, one run each in the same Blender on High: a ramen stall in the rain with a moped riding through. Carbon (in Cursor) made the cleanest stall for $2.78 but took about 4 hours of wall clock, much of it provider errors; Fable 5.2 had mirror puddles but a dark stall ($18.60 est., 39m); Opus 5.5 had real steam ($7.54 est., 27m).
- **Media:** Video, 15 s. About 287 views and 5 likes as of 12 November 2026.
- **Tool:** Cursor
- **Numbers the author reported:** $2.78 in Cursor credits · ~4h wall clock · $18.60 est. · 39m · $7.54 est. · 27m
- **Compared with:** Claude Fable 5.2, Claude Opus 5.5. Carbon cost the least and won the stall.
- **Prompt:** only the creator's request or a paraphrase is public; it is not the full prompt.
- **Note:** Carbon ran in Cursor; Fable and Opus ran in Claude Code. All Python, procedural materials only.

**The creator's request, as posted (not a full prompt)**

a ramen stall in the rain, with a moped riding through.
