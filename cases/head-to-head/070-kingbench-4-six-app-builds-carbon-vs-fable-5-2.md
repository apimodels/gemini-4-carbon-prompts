# KingBench 4: six app builds, Carbon vs Fable 5.2

Gemini 4 Carbon case 70 of 223 · Head-to-Head Tests · [简体中文](#中文)

By AICodeKing · [original YouTube page](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SvXR3bNktMA) · 2026-11-11 · [see it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/head-to-head)

- **What it is:** KingBench 4 scored six app builds, including a Pokemon Red build and a 3D room designer: Carbon placed 8th of 13 models at 53.3%, while Fable 5.2 was first at 70%. Every one of Carbon's six final messages claimed at least one feature the reviewer found broken or overstated, because Carbon only tested via its own tests and never clicked through the apps.
- **Where:** YouTube
- **Numbers the author reported:** Carbon 32/60 (53.3%), 8th of 13 models · Fable 5.2 first at 70% · Argon 54.3% on the same builds · Pokemon Red 8.2 vs 8.4 · 3D room designer 6.3 vs 9.2 · Carbon's six builds about $23 and 76M tokens vs Fable 212M tokens and about $222
- **Compared with:** Fable 5.2, Argon. Fable when it has to work; Carbon for fast first drafts that cost little and that you check yourself.
- **Prompt:** not published here. We do not reconstruct unpublished prompts.

To run Carbon yourself: [how to use Gemini 4 Carbon](https://apimodels.app/how-to-use-gemini-4-carbon) covers where it works, the settings that matter and what it costs.
