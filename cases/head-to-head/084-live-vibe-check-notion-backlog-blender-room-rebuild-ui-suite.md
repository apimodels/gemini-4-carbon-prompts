# Live vibe check: Notion backlog, Blender room rebuild, UI suite

Gemini 4 Carbon case 84 of 223 · Head-to-Head Tests · [简体中文](#中文)

By Every (Dan Shipper and team) · [original YouTube page](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G8rzzawst4s) · 2026-11-10 · [see it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/head-to-head)

- **What it is:** In a livestream, Carbon worked a frozen ~100-ticket Notion backlog, rebuilt the podcast room in Blender/Three.js/Unity over ~24 h (repeatedly crashing a Mac Studio), built a UI suite and took a Miami travel test, among other tasks. As reported by BigGo, it finished 15-16 tickets in ~4.5 h (others did 8-9 in under an hour), caught a clipped audio line and opened a PR that was merged, made a ginkgo-leaf macOS icon judged best of all models, and was the first Google model to pass Shipper's coffee-shop travel test.
- **Where:** YouTube
- **Numbers the author reported:** 15-16 tickets from a frozen ~100-ticket Notion backlog over ~4.5 h · others 8-9 tickets in under an hour · ~24 h Blender/Three.js/Unity rebuild
- **Compared with:** Fable 5.2. Mixed: its racing game was below Fable 5.2, but its Typeform-clone landing page was more distinctive than Fable's generic one.
