# Neon arcade racer, night-market voxel diorama and channel intro

Gemini 4 Carbon case 72 of 223 · Head-to-Head Tests · [简体中文](#中文)

By KostiKoNeuro · [original GitHub page](https://github.com/KostiKoNeuro/gemini-carbon-vs-fable-5-2) · 2026-11-11 · [see it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/head-to-head)

- **What it is:** Carbon in Antigravity (High) and Fable 5.2 in Claude Code 2.1.321 (High) each built a neon arcade racer, a Shenzhen night-market voxel diorama and a channel intro made in OpenMontage. Carbon won the race and the diorama (judged blind) for a 2:1 result; Fable won the intro, which is now the channel's real intro.
- **Where:** GitHub
- **Tool:** Antigravity (High)
- **Numbers the author reported:** Carbon ~2 h 07 min, $7.54 (up to $12.36 counting the Flash browser helper) · Fable ~2 h 05 min, $41.71 · paid image/video generation capped at $1 · Carbon's first race attempt broke 3 times on network timeouts ($0.68, excluded) · 2:1 to Carbon
- **Compared with:** Fable 5.2. Carbon won the race and the diorama (judged blind); Fable won the intro.
- **Prompt:** published by the creator; at 445 characters it is excerpted below, with the full text at the source.
- **Note:** Prompts were written in Russian; the quoted race prompt is the author's English translation (all translations in prompts/en/). Fable used a headless Playwright browser. A Russian video is promised.
- **Also:** [kostikoneuro.github.io/gemini-carbon-vs-fable-5-2/](https://kostikoneuro.github.io/gemini-carbon-vs-fable-5-2/)

**Prompt (excerpt; full text at the [source](https://github.com/KostiKoNeuro/gemini-carbon-vs-fable-5-2))**

```text
Make a browser arcade racer "Neon Night Racer": a night highway, a neon synthwave city, the 80s. The main thing is the feeling of insane speed at night, when one mistake means a crash. Imagine it's a game for a real arcade cabinet: you want to play it again. Surprise me. Music and sounds in code, no external files.
[…]
```
