# Three hard builds with full cost receipts across four models

Gemini 4 Carbon case 69 of 223 · Head-to-Head Tests · [简体中文](#中文)

By Pat Simmons (AI for Mortals) · [original YouTube page](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JC1Mjsifz0) · 2026-11-11 · [see it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/head-to-head)

- **What it is:** Three hard builds, one run each: a pixel-for-pixel clone of agrumeafarm.it, a 30-45 s launch film for Motion, and a browser 1940s dogfight flight sim with a Blender coastline (plus a bonus Eiffel Tower build). Carbon in Antigravity cost the least in total of the four models but ranked 4th, 4th and 3rd.
- **Where:** YouTube
- **Tool:** Antigravity
- **Numbers the author reported:** Carbon $27.68 / 269 min · Fable $883.30 / 1,024 min · Opus $181.29 / 564 min · Astra $126.43 / 181 min (API-equivalent) · Carbon ranked 4th, 4th and 3rd
- **Compared with:** Fable 5.2, Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Astra. A clear step up from Argon that costs much less, but behind Opus and Fable; the author still prefers Opus 5.5.
- **Prompt:** not published here. We do not reconstruct unpublished prompts.
- **Note:** Astra ran in Codex, Opus and Fable in Claude Code. Prompts are published in full on the blog (each well over 600 characters).
- **Also:** [www.aiformortals.co/blog/gemini-4-carbon-review](https://www.aiformortals.co/blog/gemini-4-carbon-review)

To run Carbon yourself: [how to use Gemini 4 Carbon](https://apimodels.app/how-to-use-gemini-4-carbon) covers where it works, the settings that matter and what it costs.
