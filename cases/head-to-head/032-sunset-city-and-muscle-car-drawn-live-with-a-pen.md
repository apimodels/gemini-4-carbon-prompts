# Sunset city and muscle car drawn live with a pen

Gemini 4 Carbon case 32 of 223 · Head-to-Head Tests · [简体中文](#中文)

By [@olezhavich](https://x.com/olezhavich) · [original post](https://x.com/olezhavich/status/2117706843799658507) · 2026-11-03 · [see it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/head-to-head)

- **What it is:** Same prompt, one try each: draw a sunset over a city in about 10 seconds, let the color flood in, and park an old American muscle car in front. The author calls Carbon's moody night city the most cinematic, Opus 5.5's skyline the most detailed, Argon's car nicely drawn and GPT-6 Astra's colors the palest.
- **Media:** Video, 11 s. About 23K views and 88 likes as of 12 November 2026.
- **Numbers the author reported:** one try each
- **Compared with:** Claude Opus 5.5, Gemini 4 Argon, GPT-6 Astra. Carbon is the most cinematic; Opus has the most detail.
- **Prompt:** only the creator's request or a paraphrase is public; it is not the full prompt.
- **Note:** Posted 3 Nov, before the 10 Nov API launch. All four models painted the car teal.

**The creator's request, as posted (not a full prompt)**

```text
draw a sunset over a city in about 10 seconds, watch the color flood in, and park an old American muscle car in front.
```

作者:[@olezhavich](https://x.com/olezhavich) · [原帖](https://x.com/olezhavich/status/2117706843799658507) · 2026-11-03 · [在 apimodels.app 案例库查看](https://apimodels.app/zh/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/head-to-head)

- **作品:** 同一提示词、每个模型跑一次：用大约 10 秒画出城市夕阳，让颜色铺开，再在前面停一辆美式老肌肉车。作者认为 Carbon 的夜色城市最有电影感，Opus 5.5 天际线最细致，Argon 车画得好，GPT-6 Astra 颜色最淡。
- **媒体:** 视频 11 秒。截至 2026-11-12 约 2.3 万 浏览、88 赞。
- **作者公布的数字:** one try each
- **对比对象:** Claude Opus 5.5、Gemini 4 Argon、GPT-6 Astra。Carbon 最有电影感；Opus 细节最多。
- **提示词:** 只公开了作者的需求或转述,不是完整提示词。
- **备注:** 发布于 11 月 3 日，早于 11 月 10 日 API 正式开放。四个模型都把车涂成了青绿色。
