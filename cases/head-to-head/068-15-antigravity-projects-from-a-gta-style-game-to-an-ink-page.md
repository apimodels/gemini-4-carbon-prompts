# 15 Antigravity projects, from a GTA-style game to an ink page

Gemini 4 Carbon case 68 of 223 · Head-to-Head Tests · [简体中文](#中文)

By 亚里 Yari · [original YouTube page](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAE66-zzOy8) · 2026-11-11 · [see it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/head-to-head)

- **What it is:** Carbon High in Google Antigravity (Turbo mode, Google AI Ultra) built 15 projects, including a GTA-style game, CS Dust2, a Minecraft sunset, promo films, a course-selection redesign, a holo card opener, a video editor, a rabies explainer and an ink web page. The verdict: strong at long tasks, average taste, and one-line wishes rarely work.
- **Where:** YouTube
- **Tool:** Google Antigravity, Carbon High, Turbo mode
- **Numbers the author reported:** GTA-style game: 34 min · CS: 36 min · self-intro: 43 min · promo: 2 rounds, ~45 and ~56 min · Minecraft rerun: 32 min in one pass (Argon took 6 rounds, ~2 h 18 min) · course system: 22 min · holo cards: 48 min · video editor: 1 h 13 min · headphone site: 1 h 49 min · rabies explainer: ~1 h 33 min (2 rounds) · ink page: ~42 min · India-city short: ~1 h 10 min · after ~5 h and 17 sessions: 75% of the 5-hour quota and 78% of the weekly quota left
- **Compared with:** Argon, Opus 5.5. Strong at long tasks with average taste; one-line wishes rarely work.
- **Prompt:** published by the creator, quoted in full below.
- **Note:** All prompts are published verbatim in each folder's PROMPT.md; the one shown is for the CS project. Two items (01_gta6, 05_minecraft-sunset) were first run on Argon and redone on Carbon; the Opus 5.5 comparison is the author's earlier GTA build.
