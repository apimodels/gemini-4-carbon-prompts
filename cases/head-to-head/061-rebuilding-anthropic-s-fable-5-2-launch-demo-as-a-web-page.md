# Rebuilding Anthropic's Fable 5.2 launch demo as a web page

Gemini 4 Carbon case 61 of 223 · Head-to-Head Tests · [简体中文](#中文)

By [@akokoi1](https://x.com/akokoi1) · [original post](https://x.com/akokoi1/status/2120309192666104110) · 2026-11-11 · [see it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/head-to-head)

- **What it is:** The author gave Carbon the video Anthropic had posted of a Fable 5.2 creation and asked it to rebuild it as a web page; the result was good. Takeaways: a big step up from earlier Gemini models, taste still behind Fable and Opus, and it needs detailed prompts or reference examples rather than one-line wishes.
- **Media:** Video, 15 s. About 4,822 views and 14 likes as of 12 November 2026.
- **Compared with:** Claude Fable 5.2. Big progress, but taste is still behind Fable and Opus.
- **Prompt:** not published here. We do not reconstruct unpublished prompts.
