# One-shot Mario-style 3D platformer via Vertex AI

Gemini 4 Carbon case 146 of 223 · Games · [简体中文](#中文)

By [@Delroy715](https://x.com/Delroy715) · [original post](https://x.com/Delroy715/status/2120140252849611036) · 2026-11-10 · [see it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/games)

- **What it is:** One shot with no external tools, through Vertex AI: a playable Mario-style 3D platformer with multi-joint skeletal animation, 4 levels, moving-platform physics, Web Audio music and 10 sound effects. It came to 2,599 lines (99.6 KB) in one HTML file, 73,907 tokens in total and 558.7 seconds end to end, at a reported bill of $0.45.
- **Media:** Video, 37 s. About 4,366 views and 13 likes as of 12 November 2026.
- **Tool:** Vertex AI
- **Numbers the author reported:** 2,599 行 / 99.6 KB · 38,289 Tokens · 35,618 Tokens · 73,907 Tokens · 558.7 秒（9 分 18 秒） · $0.45
- **Prompt:** not published here. We do not reconstruct unpublished prompts.
- **Note:** Billed at the promo rates valid until 30 Nov. @KOF97AI later posted a near-identical write-up with the same figures but said it ran in Antigravity. The quoted earlier post by this author compares Carbon and Argon on a one-line pelican SVG prompt via Vertex AI.

To run Carbon yourself: [how to use Gemini 4 Carbon](https://apimodels.app/how-to-use-gemini-4-carbon) covers where it works, the settings that matter and what it costs.
