# Rebuilding a Fable-made shooter from its gameplay video

Gemini 4 Carbon case 142 of 223 · Games · [简体中文](#中文)

By [@mylifcc](https://x.com/mylifcc) · [original post](https://x.com/mylifcc/status/2120351887979647076) · 2026-11-11 · [see it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/games)

- **What it is:** The author fed Carbon in Antigravity the gameplay video of Saltline, a 3D web shooter Fable 5.2 built in an 8-hour, $550 run, and asked for an identical or better game. After 48 minutes and $3.82 it produced a 638.5KB single HTML file with Three.js inlined plus new mechanics; a visual self-check loop with headless Chrome screenshots caught and fixed three bugs, such as a barrel rotated 22.5 degrees.
- **Media:** Video, 52 s. About 7,165 views and 33 likes as of 12 November 2026.
- **Tool:** Antigravity
- **Numbers the author reported:** 48 分钟后 · 3.82 美元 · 7.64 美元 · 638.5KB · 103KB · 39 轮调用（562.5 万总输入 / 468.4 万命中缓存 / 14.7 万输出 token） · 8 小时、烧掉 550 美元
- **Prompt:** only the creator's request or a paraphrase is public; it is not the full prompt.
- **Note:** Saltline was made with Fable 5.2 by Anthropic engineer Felix Rieseberg. Not like-for-like: that run started from a three-line prompt, while Carbon got the finished game's video. $7.64 is the author's estimate at the post-promo price.

**The creator's request, as posted (not a full prompt)**

```text
能不能做一个一模一样，或者正面超越它的东西？
```
