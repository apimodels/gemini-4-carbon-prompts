# Open-world pelican bike game across Google's campus

Gemini 4 Carbon case 140 of 223 · Games · [简体中文](#中文)

By [@agenticjay](https://x.com/agenticjay) · [original post](https://x.com/agenticjay/status/2120209797639524587) · 2026-11-10 · [see it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/games)

- **What it is:** The author took the pelican-riding-a-bicycle benchmark into a playable game using Carbon and /swarm in Antigravity. Multi-agent critique loops ran in 5 waves: subagents generated Nano Banana and Omni references, rigged assets in headless Blender, wired Three.js bike physics, wrote custom WebGL2 shaders and added procedural Web Audio effects with a Lyria track.
- **Media:** Video, 77 s. About 1,249 views and 55 likes as of 12 November 2026.
- **Tool:** Antigravity
- **Numbers the author reported:** 5 waves
- **Prompt:** not published here. We do not reconstruct unpublished prompts.
- **Note:** The author calls it "our Mountain View campus"; the video is the one in Sundar Pichai's launch post. Reposted by many accounts (@choblin29, @alexmichael, @EnvolDev, @masahirochaen, @Saboo_Shubham_, @songguoxiansen); this is the post where the maker describes it.

To run Carbon yourself: [how to use Gemini 4 Carbon](https://apimodels.app/how-to-use-gemini-4-carbon) covers where it works, the settings that matter and what it costs.
