# Carbon splitting a scene into tasks for a local model

Gemini 4 Carbon case 214 of 223 · Agents, Research and Writing · [简体中文](#中文)

By [@atomicagent_io](https://x.com/atomicagent_io) · [original post](https://x.com/atomicagent_io/status/2120227531504918833) · 2026-11-10 · [see it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/research)

- **What it is:** In Atomic Agent Fusion, Carbon split the prompt into tasks and sent them to a local Qwen 3.8 27B. The minion scene took 5 minutes and $2.39, versus 16 minutes and $7.71 with Carbon alone.
- **Media:** Video, 30 s. About 13K views and 140 likes as of 12 November 2026.
- **Numbers the author reported:** 5 min and $2.39 · 16 min and $7.71
- **Compared with:** Gemini 4 Carbon alone. Orchestrating a local model was about 3x faster at about a third of the cost.
- **Prompt:** not published here. We do not reconstruct unpublished prompts.
- **Note:** Promotional post by the Atomic Agent team, run in their own orchestration product.

To run Carbon yourself: [how to use Gemini 4 Carbon](https://apimodels.app/how-to-use-gemini-4-carbon) covers where it works, the settings that matter and what it costs.
