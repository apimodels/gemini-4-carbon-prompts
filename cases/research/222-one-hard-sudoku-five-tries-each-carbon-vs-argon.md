# One hard Sudoku, five tries each: Carbon vs Argon

Gemini 4 Carbon case 222 of 223 · Agents, Research and Writing · [简体中文](#中文)

By Singularity Feed (GitHub gnana70) · [original YouTube page](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nRiEC1B2f4) · 2026-11-11 · [see it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/research)

- **What it is:** Through the Gemini API (Interactions) at thinking_level high, Carbon and Argon each tried one hard Sudoku five times, with answers checked by code. Both got 5/5 right; Carbon was a little quicker, with tokens and cost about the same.
- **Where:** YouTube
- **Tool:** Gemini API (Interactions), thinking_level high
- **Numbers the author reported:** both 5/5 correct · minutes per try: Carbon 6.3-9.6 (median 6.6) vs Argon 6.4-13.5 (median 8.2) · thinking tokens avg 46,857 vs 47,960 · cost avg $0.56 vs $0.57 per try · ten tries about $5.60
- **Compared with:** Argon. Carbon was a little quicker; tokens and cost were about the same.
- **Prompt:** not published here. We do not reconstruct unpublished prompts.
- **Note:** Tested 2026-11-10, published 2026-11-11. The prompt is the PROMPT constant in the published script on GitHub.
- **Also:** [github.com/gnana70/gemini-4-carbon-sudoku-test](https://github.com/gnana70/gemini-4-carbon-sudoku-test)
