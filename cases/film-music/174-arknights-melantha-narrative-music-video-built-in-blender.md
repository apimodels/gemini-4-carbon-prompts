# Arknights "Melantha" narrative music video built in Blender

Gemini 4 Carbon case 174 of 223 · Films, Music Videos and Sound · [简体中文](#中文)

By xplunger1337 · [original Bilibili page](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1PSWD6LE6J) · 2026-11-12 · [see it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/film-music)

- **What it is:** Carbon made a PV-style narrative music video in Blender about the Arknights operator Melantha's view of war and conflict, using Nano Banana for images and "Gemini Flow Omni 1.2" for detail. The author found minimal one-shot prompts gave mediocre results, while complex prompts plus multiple turns worked well.
- **Where:** Bilibili
- **Tool:** Blender, plus Nano Banana for images and "Gemini Flow Omni 1.2" for detail
- **Prompt:** published by the creator, quoted in full below.
- **Note:** The quoted prompt is the author's revised first-turn prompt.

**Prompt (the creator's own words)**

```text
请使用blender制作一个叙事性mv动画，风格偏向pv短片，背景音乐使用ref.mp3。2.动画主题是在明日方舟世界观，以干员玫兰莎视角看待战争与冲突。3.自由发挥，质量尽可能最好，4.不要吝啬token和工具调用，分镜连贯流畅，需要什么工具直接下，5。玫兰莎的参考设定图在文件夹里。可以用nanobanana来生图或者修改网上搜到的图，尽量风格化一下让ai图不那么出戏。6.可以使用gemini flow的omni 1.2来补充细节（仅在必要时） 7.你具有决策权，只是不要做成那种“ai味很重的，看起来一股廉价web感”的mv。一切质量优先。如果你不确定，可以先去看看何为ai味视频的讨论和案例 ，并以pv关键词在bilibili搜索视频学习何为优秀案例。
```
