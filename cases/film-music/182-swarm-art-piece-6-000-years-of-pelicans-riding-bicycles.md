# /swarm art piece: 6,000 years of pelicans riding bicycles

Gemini 4 Carbon case 182 of 223 · Films, Music Videos and Sound · [简体中文](#中文)

By orbitooo · [original Bilibili page](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1SsWy6TEKx) · 2026-11-11 · [see it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/film-music)

- **What it is:** In Antigravity (Agy 2.x) on the official subscription, /swarm was told to watch a Bilibili video, save its original prompt and create an art piece; a main agent and 33 subagents delivered a "6,000 years of pelicans riding bicycles" web piece.
- **Where:** Bilibili
- **Tool:** Antigravity (Agy 2.x) on the official subscription, /swarm
- **Numbers the author reported:** main agent + 33 subagents · 336.99M tokens total · about $212.93
- **Compared with:** Opus 5.5. The author thinks it beats the earlier Opus 5.5 version.
- **Prompt:** published by the creator, quoted in full below.
- **Note:** The author also says Agy is still hard to use.
- **Also:** [pelican.orbitoo.top/](https://pelican.orbitoo.top/)
