# Interactive 19th-century Parisian physics lab from one reference image

Gemini 4 Carbon case 157 of 223 · Simulations, Science and Engineering · [简体中文](#中文)

By [@thebuggeddev](https://x.com/thebuggeddev) · [original post](https://x.com/thebuggeddev/status/2120201759305998343) · 2026-11-10 · [see it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/simulations)

- **What it is:** From just a reference image, Carbon built an interactive 19th-century-style Parisian physics laboratory: 92 machined brass, copper and glass parts, a live Beer-Lambert Rankine vortex, an exploded view and an archival UI with a 60 FPS SVG wattmeter. The author says it designed the whole UI with no design references.
- **Media:** Video, 48 s. About 51K views and 345 likes as of 12 November 2026.
- **Numbers the author reported:** 92 machined brass, copper, and borosilicate parts
