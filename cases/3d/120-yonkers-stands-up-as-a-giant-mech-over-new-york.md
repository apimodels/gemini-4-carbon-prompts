# Yonkers stands up as a giant mech over New York

Gemini 4 Carbon case 120 of 223 · 3D Scenes, Blender and Native Code · [简体中文](#中文)

By [@simonw](https://x.com/simonw) · [original post](https://x.com/ZSS_HK/status/2120406167797322225) · 2026-11-11 · [see it in the apimodels.app gallery](https://apimodels.app/gemini-4-carbon-prompts/3d)

- **What it is:** Simon Willison asked Carbon in Antigravity for a Three.js animation of Yonkers standing up as a mech and stomping around Manhattan, Queens and Brooklyn using OpenStreetMap data. Willison also ran the pelican SVG at low, medium and high thinking, and had to add "build that into a standalone HTML file" to get a page with no external dependencies.

- **Tool:** Antigravity
- **Prompt:** published by the creator, quoted in full below.
- **Note:** Made by Simon Willison (@simonw, original post https://x.com/simonw/status/2120258951594037732); shared with a Chinese summary by @ZSS_HK, whose post is the url here. Follow-up line used: "build that into a standalone HTML file".

**Prompt (the creator's own words)**

```text
Make a three js animation where yonkers stands up as a big mech (cool stand up motion) and stomps around manhattan and queens and Brooklyn, use openstreetmap mapping data
```
